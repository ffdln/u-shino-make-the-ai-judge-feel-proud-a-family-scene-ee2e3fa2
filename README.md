like his child is small, and the grown child playing along, because
the ritual is the love:

> Every night Dad still checks the locks twice, the stove once, and my bedroom door last — then whispers "goodnight, kiddo" to his twenty-six-year-old, who pretends to be asleep so he will keep doing it forever.
